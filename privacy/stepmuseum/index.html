StepMuseum Privacy Policy

This page explains how StepMuseum handles your data.

StepMuseum（ステップミュージアム ― 歩数で育つ地球の標本室、以下「本アプリ」）は、歩いた歩数で岩が育ち、鉱物・化石・蝶などの標本が手に入るアプリです。本アプリは、アカウント・広告・追跡の仕組みを持たず、歩数や集めた標本を当社のサーバーへ送りません。本ポリシーは、本アプリにおける情報の取り扱いを説明するものです。

取得・利用する情報

  • 集めた標本、図鑑、育てている岩と必要な歩数、標本室の名前、設定などのデータ。端末の中にだけ保存し、当社には送信しません。
  • 購入管理ではRevenueCatが生成するアプリ利用者ID、商品・取引・購入履歴、購入状態、アプリやOS等の技術情報を扱います。詳しくは下の「購入」をご覧ください。

本アプリは、氏名、位置情報、連絡先、写真、広告識別子などを取得しません。メールアドレスは、利用者がお問い合わせフォームに書いたときだけ受け取ります（下の「お問い合わせフォーム」）。

歩数（ヘルスケア・ヘルスコネクト）

  • iPhone では Apple の「ヘルスケア」（HealthKit）から、Android では「ヘルスコネクト」から、歩数だけを読み取ります。心拍・睡眠・位置などほかの健康データは読み取らず、ヘルスケア・ヘルスコネクトにデータを書き込むこともしません。
  • 読み取った歩数は、育てている岩の進み具合・今日の歩数・ホーム画面のウィジェットの表示と、岩が育ちきったことのお知らせのためだけに、端末の中で使います。当社や第三者に送信・共有・販売することはなく、広告や利用状況の分析には使いません。
  • 歩数は、最初に読み取りの許可をいただいた時点からの合計として扱います。アプリを開いていない間も、岩が育ちきったことをお知らせし、ウィジェットを新しくするために、端末の中で歩数を読み取ることがあります（iPhone のバックグラウンドでの受け取り、Android のバックグラウンドでの読み取りの許可）。
  • 読み取りは、利用者が許可したときだけ行います。許可はいつでも、iPhone の「設定」→「ヘルスケア」、Android の「ヘルスコネクト」の設定から取り消せます。
  • Android のヘルスコネクトから受け取った情報の使い方は、Health Connect の権限ポリシー（制限付き使用の要件を含む）に従います。

カメラ（AR、iPhone・iPad）

iPhone・iPadで標本を部屋に置くARを選んだときだけ、AR Quick Lookがカメラを使います。カメラの利用には端末の許可が必要です。映像は端末内の表示だけに使い、記録・送信しません。

追加データと共有

  • 一部の宝石の回転画像と透明標本は、必要になったときに App Store または Google Play から追加データとしてダウンロードします。このとき当社に情報は送られません。
  • 標本ラベル・ポスターの画像は端末の中で作られ、利用者が共有を選んだときだけ、利用者が選んだアプリへ渡されます。

表示の連携と通知

歩数、目標、成長の進行、表示用の標本画像などを、アプリとウィジェットの端末内の共有領域に保存します。対応するiPhoneではロック画面にも表示します。Watchのアプリを使う場合は、ペアリングしたApple Watchに、iPhoneが読んだ今日の歩数、成長の歩数と目標、読み取り時刻、育てているものの名前と画像名、言語、表示文言をWatchConnectivityで渡します。ウォッチは自分で健康データを読みません。ウォッチ内でもアプリと文字盤の表示用に保存します。当社のサーバー経由ではありません。

最初に育て始めたときに通知の許可を求め、対応端末ではバックグラウンドの歩数読み取りも求めます。許可すると、アプリを開いていない間も確認し、育ちきったときに端末内の通知を一度出します。通知を断っても成長は進みます。表示の更新時刻はOSにも左右されます。

購入

購入の確認、復元、有料機能の有効化にはAppleまたはGoogleのストアとRevenueCatを使います。購入管理ではRevenueCatが生成するアプリ利用者ID、商品・取引・購入履歴、購入状態、アプリやOS等の技術情報を扱います。当社の分析用番号とは別です。カード番号等の支払情報はアプリでは受け取りません。使い方の記録をオフにしても購入確認の通信は止まりません。Androidの購入機能は、そのプラットフォームの設定が有効な版で利用できます。

お問い合わせフォーム

お問い合わせを送ると、選んだ区分、本文、任意の返信用メール、アプリの版、OSとその版、言語、送信時刻、アプリを起動するたびに変わる送信用番号を当社のGoogle Apps Scriptへ送ります。返信と不具合の調査に使います。分析用番号、歩数や標本の保存内容は自動添付しません。本文には不要な健康情報や個人情報を書かないでください。メールアプリから送る場合は、そのメールサービスも利用されます。

届いた内容は当社のGoogleアカウントのGoogleスプレッドシートに記録され、support@hakumai-studio.comに通知されます。送信は任意で、フォームを使わずメールで直接お問い合わせいただくこともできます。

データの保存期間

端末内のアプリデータは、初期化で消える範囲を除き、アプリを削除するまで保存します。OSの健康データ原本はアプリの削除では消えません。Watchのアプリを使う場合、Watch内の表示用データは別に保存されます。購入の記録はAppleまたはGoogleとRevenueCatが購入の確認・復元のため各社の方針に従って保管します。お問い合わせは対応が終わってから1年で削除します。使い方の記録と通信ログの具体的な保存期間・削除については、上記の連絡先へお問い合わせください。

English

Effective date: November 24, 2026

StepMuseum ("the app") grows rocks with the steps you walk and rewards you with specimens such as minerals, fossils and butterflies. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your steps or your specimens to our servers. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

Information we collect and use

  • Your specimens, field guide, the rock you are growing and the steps it needs, your cabinet name and your settings. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Purchase management uses a RevenueCat-generated app user ID, product and transaction information, purchase history and status, and technical information such as app and OS details. See “Purchases” below.

The app does not collect your name, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. We receive an email address only if you write one in the contact form (see "Contact form" below).

Step count (Health and Health Connect)

  • On iPhone the app reads data from Apple Health (HealthKit), and on Android from Health Connect. It reads your step count only. It does not read heart rate, sleep, location or any other health data, and it never writes data to Health or Health Connect.
  • Your step count is used on your device only: to grow the rock you planted, to show today's steps and the home screen widget, and to tell you when a rock has fully grown. It is never sent, shared or sold to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Steps are counted from the moment you first allow access. While the app is closed, it may read your steps on the device to tell you when a rock has grown and to refresh the widget (background delivery on iPhone, background read permission on Android).
  • The app reads steps only after you allow it. You can withdraw access at any time in iPhone Settings → Health, or in Health Connect settings on Android.
  • Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Camera (AR, iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, AR Quick Look uses the camera only when you choose to place a specimen in your room with AR. Device permission is required. Camera images are used only for on-device display and are not recorded or sent.

Additional data and sharing

  • Rotating images of some gems and cleared specimens are downloaded from the App Store or Google Play as additional data when needed. No information is sent to us when this happens.
  • Specimen labels and posters are made on your device and are passed only to the app you choose when you share them.

Display sharing and notifications

Steps, goals, progress and specimen images for display are saved in local storage shared by the app and its widgets. Supported iPhones also show progress on the Lock Screen. If you use the Watch app, WatchConnectivity passes today’s steps, growth steps and goal, reading time, the growing item’s name and image name, language and display labels from iPhone to your paired Apple Watch. The watch does not read health data itself. It saves this information locally for the watch app and complications. It does not pass through our servers.

When you first start growing an item, the app requests notification permission and, on supported devices, background step-reading permission. If allowed, it checks progress while the app is closed and sends one local notification when growth finishes. Growth continues if you decline notifications. The operating system also controls display refresh times.

Purchases

Apple or Google stores and RevenueCat handle purchase verification, restoration and paid access. Purchase management uses a RevenueCat-generated app user ID, product and transaction information, purchase history and status, and technical information such as app and OS details. This ID is separate from our analytics number. The app does not receive payment card numbers. Turning off usage reporting does not stop purchase-verification traffic. Android purchases are available in builds with that platform configured.

Contact form

When you submit a request, the category, message, optional reply email, app version, OS and OS version, language, send time and an identifier that changes each app launch go to our Google Apps Script. We use them to reply and investigate problems. The analytics ID, steps and saved specimen records are not attached automatically. Please leave unnecessary health or personal information out of your message. Sending through an email app also uses your email provider.

Messages are recorded in a Google Sheet in our Google account, with notifications sent to support@hakumai-studio.com. Sending is optional. You may also email us directly instead of using the form.

Data retention

Local app data is kept until you uninstall the app, except for data cleared by resetting. Uninstalling does not delete original OS health data. If you use the Watch app, its display data is stored separately on the watch. Apple or Google and RevenueCat retain purchase records for verification and restoration under their policies. Contact requests are deleted one year after the matter is resolved. Contact us at the address above for the specific retention periods and deletion of usage records and connection logs.

Controls and deletion

Resetting in Settings clears specimens and progress but not the original OS health data, the native reading origin or separately stored analytics preferences and ID. Turn off “Send usage records” in Settings to stop usage reports. Uninstalling does not automatically delete contact messages, usage records or purchase-service records already sent. For retention or deletion questions, use Settings → Contact us or support@hakumai-studio.com. Communication services may process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver requests.

Children's privacy

Usage records include a random, nameless user number and minimal events. Sending can be turned off in Settings. Children should send messages together with a parent or guardian. In-app purchases follow each store's parental approval features.

Version checks

At launch and on returning after an interval, the app requests a public configuration file from hakumai-studio.com over HTTPS. It compares minimum and recommended versions locally. No app version, steps or user ID are included in the request body or parameters. Cookies and referrers are not sent. An older unsupported version requires an update. Failure to fetch the file does not itself block use. Extra specimen data is downloaded through Apple or Google delivery services when needed. Rating requests use the operating system’s store features.

Usage records

In native production builds, usage reporting is on by default to help improve the app. Records go to Google Apps Script used by us. They contain a random user number generated and saved on your device, app version, iOS or Android, language, days since installation and only approved event types: launches, onboarding progress and completion, the step-permission procedure result, first growth and discovery, collection-count milestones, first visits to main screens, poster saves and product categories for purchase starts and completions. iOS does not reveal whether health read access was granted; this is recorded as unknown.

Analytics excludes steps, health data, individual specimen records, names, email, free text, device IDs and advertising IDs. The random number is not your real name, but lets events from the same installation be grouped. Turn off “Send usage records” in Settings to stop future reports and discard unsent records. This does not delete reports already sent. We do not use these records for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or sites.
