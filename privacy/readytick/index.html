日本語

施行日：2026年10月16日

ReadyTick（レディティック、以下「本アプリ」）は、朝、家を出るまでの残り時間とやることを見せ、準備のあいだだけ SNS を止める準備のアプリです。本アプリは、アカウント・広告・利用状況の分析・追跡の仕組みを持ちません。当社が受け取るのは、利用者が自分で送ったお問い合わせだけです。本ポリシーは、本アプリにおける情報の取り扱いを説明するものです。

端末の中だけで使う情報

  • 朝のパターン（起床・出発の時刻、曜日、やること）、今日だけの変更、準備の記録（いつ何を済ませたか、出発した時刻）、それをもとにした見積もりと集計、設定。端末の中に保存し、当社には送信しません。
  • ロック画面とダイナミックアイランドの表示（ライブアクティビティ）、ホーム画面のウィジェット、残り時間の通知、起床アラーム、声での読み上げは、すべて端末の中で処理します。
  • SNS制限（スクリーンタイム）：制限するアプリの選択と、制限をかける・外す処理には、Apple のスクリーンタイムの仕組み（Family Controls・Managed Settings・Device Activity）を使います。選んだアプリは Apple が作る見えない記号として扱われ、本アプリも当社も、どのアプリを選んだか、どのアプリをどれだけ使ったかを知ることはできません。これらの情報は端末の外に出ません。

本アプリは、氏名、位置情報、連絡先、写真、健康のデータ、広告識別子、端末の識別子などを取得しません。

お問い合わせ

設定の「お問い合わせ」から送ると、次の内容を当社が受け取ります：種類（不具合・ご意見・その他）、本文、返信用のメールアドレス（任意）、アプリの版、OSの版、表示言語、送った日時、アプリを起動するたびに作り直す乱数のID（連続して送られるのを防ぐため。端末には保存せず、広告や端末の識別子ではありません）。これらは Google のスプレッドシートに保存され、通知メールとして当社に届きます。返信とアプリの改善のためにだけ使い、ほかの目的には使いません。

新しい版の確認

本アプリは起動時に、新しい版が出ているかを確かめるため、当社のウェブサイト（hakumai-studio.com）から小さな設定ファイルを読み込みます。このとき本アプリから利用者の情報は送りません。ただし、通信の仕組み上、ウェブサイトの配信事業者（GitHub）にIPアドレスなどの接続記録が残ることがあります。当社はこの記録を、利用者の特定や分析に使いません。

アプリの評価

本アプリは、App Store の評価の画面を、Apple の仕組みを使って出すことがあります。評価の内容は Apple が扱い、当社には利用者の情報は届きません。

利用目的

  • 朝の準備の表示、残り時間のお知らせ、起床アラーム、SNS制限
  • 準備の記録をもとにした見積もりと集計の表示
  • 新しい版のお知らせ
  • お問い合わせへの返信と、アプリの改善

処理の法的根拠（EEA・英国の利用者向け）

お問い合わせへの返信は、利用者の求めに応じるための正当な利益に基づきます。朝の準備に関する情報は端末の中に保存され、当社は処理しません。

第三者サービス

本アプリは、SNS制限のために Apple のスクリーンタイムの仕組みを、起床アラームのために Apple の AlarmKit を、評価の画面のために Apple の StoreKit を利用します。ウェブサイトの配信には GitHub Pages を、お問い合わせの受け取りには Google（Google Apps Script・Google スプレッドシート）を利用しています。これらのサービスで処理される情報は、それぞれのプライバシーポリシーに従います。有料版の購入を始めるときは、このページを更新してお知らせします。

データの保存期間

端末内に保存される情報は、本アプリの設定の「記録を削除」「すべて初期化」で消せます。また、本アプリを削除すると、端末内の情報もすべて消えます。お問い合わせの内容は、対応が済んだあとも必要な期間だけ保存し、ご依頼があれば削除します。

お子さまのプライバシー

本アプリは、お問い合わせで送られた内容のほかは、お子さまを含め、どなたからも個人情報を取得しません。お問い合わせを消してほしいときは、下の連絡先までお知らせください。

本ポリシーの変更

本ポリシーを変更する場合は、このページと施行日を更新します。

English

Effective date: October 16, 2026

ReadyTick ("the app") helps you get ready in the morning: it shows the minutes left until you leave and what is left to do, and pauses social media apps only while you get ready. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us through the contact form. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

Information used only on your device

  • Your morning plans (wake and leave times, weekdays and tasks), one-day changes, your get-ready history (what you finished and when, and when you left), the estimates and summaries based on it, and your settings. This is stored on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island display (Live Activity), Home Screen widgets, reminder notifications, the wake-up alarm and spoken announcements are all handled on your device.
  • Social media limits (Screen Time): choosing which apps to limit, and turning limits on and off, use Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). The apps you choose are handled as opaque tokens created by Apple, so neither the app nor we can know which apps you chose or how much you used them. This information never leaves your device.

The app does not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifier or device identifiers.

Contact form

When you send a message from Contact us in the settings, we receive: the type (problem, idea or other), your message, a reply email address (optional), the app version, the OS version, the display language, the time it was sent, and a random ID created anew each time the app starts (to prevent repeated sending; it is not stored on your device and is not an advertising or device identifier). This is stored in a Google spreadsheet and sent to us as a notification email. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

Checking for new versions

When it starts, the app reads a small settings file from our website (hakumai-studio.com) to check whether a new version is available. The app sends no information about you with this request. As with any web request, the website's hosting provider (GitHub) may keep connection records such as your IP address. We do not use these records to identify or analyse users.

App ratings

The app may show the App Store rating prompt using Apple's system. Ratings are handled by Apple, and no information about you is sent to us.

Purposes of use

  • Showing your morning, reminding you of the time left, the wake-up alarm and social media limits
  • Showing estimates and summaries based on your get-ready history
  • Letting you know about new versions
  • Replying to your messages and improving the app

Data controller

The provider and data controller of the app is:

Company: Hakumai Studio
Address: 4F Kabutocho No. 6 Hayama Building, 17-2 Nihonbashi Kabutocho, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 103-0026, Japan
Contact: support@hakumai-studio.com

Legal basis for processing (users in the EEA and the UK)

Replying to your messages is based on our legitimate interest in responding to your request. Information about your mornings is stored on your device and is not processed by us.

Third-party services

The app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks for social media limits, Apple's AlarmKit for the wake-up alarm and Apple's StoreKit for the rating prompt. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and contact messages are received with Google (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets). Information processed by these services is subject to their own privacy policies. If we start offering paid features, we will update this page.

Data retention

Information stored on your device can be removed with "Delete history" or "Reset everything" in the app's settings, and all of it is deleted when you delete the app. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed after we have responded, and are deleted on request.

Children's privacy

Apart from what you send through the contact form, the app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. To have a message deleted, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

Contact

support@hakumai-studio.com

한국어

시행일: 2026년 10월 16일

ReadyTick(레디틱, 이하 "본 앱")은 아침에 집을 나서기까지 남은 시간과 할 일을 보여 주고, 준비하는 동안에만 SNS를 멈추는 준비 앱이에요. 본 앱에는 계정, 광고, 이용 분석, 추적 기능이 없어요. 저희가 받는 정보는 이용자가 직접 보낸 문의뿐이에요. 이 방침은 본 앱에서 정보를 어떻게 다루는지 설명해요.

기기 안에서만 쓰는 정보

  • 아침 패턴(기상·출발 시각, 요일, 할 일), 하루만 바꾼 내용, 준비 기록(무엇을 언제 마쳤는지, 출발한 시각), 이를 바탕으로 한 예상 시간과 집계, 설정. 기기 안에 저장하며 저희에게 보내지 않아요.
  • 잠금 화면과 다이내믹 아일랜드 표시(라이브 액티비티), 홈 화면 위젯, 남은 시간 알림, 기상 알람, 음성 안내는 모두 기기 안에서 처리해요.
  • SNS 제한(스크린 타임): 제한할 앱 선택과 제한을 걸고 푸는 처리에는 Apple의 스크린 타임 기능(Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity)을 써요. 고른 앱은 Apple이 만든 알아볼 수 없는 기호로 다뤄지기 때문에, 본 앱도 저희도 어떤 앱을 골랐는지, 어떤 앱을 얼마나 썼는지 알 수 없어요. 이 정보는 기기 밖으로 나가지 않아요.

본 앱은 이름, 위치 정보, 연락처, 사진, 건강 데이터, 광고 식별자, 기기 식별자 등을 수집하지 않아요.

문의

설정의 "문의하기"에서 보내면 저희는 다음 내용을 받아요: 종류(문제·의견·기타), 본문, 답장 받을 이메일(선택), 앱 버전, OS 버전, 표시 언어, 보낸 시각, 앱을 실행할 때마다 새로 만드는 무작위 ID(연속 전송을 막기 위한 것. 기기에 저장하지 않으며 광고나 기기 식별자가 아니에요). 이 정보는 Google 스프레드시트에 저장되고 알림 메일로 저희에게 전달돼요. 답장과 앱 개선에만 쓰고 다른 목적에는 쓰지 않아요.

새 버전 확인

본 앱은 실행할 때 새 버전이 나왔는지 확인하려고 저희 웹사이트(hakumai-studio.com)에서 작은 설정 파일을 읽어요. 이때 본 앱은 이용자의 정보를 보내지 않아요. 다만 통신 구조상 웹사이트 호스팅 업체(GitHub)에 IP 주소 등의 접속 기록이 남을 수 있어요. 저희는 이 기록을 이용자를 특정하거나 분석하는 데 쓰지 않아요.

앱 평가

본 앱은 Apple의 기능으로 App Store 평가 화면을 띄울 수 있어요. 평가 내용은 Apple이 다루며, 저희에게 이용자의 정보는 전달되지 않아요.
