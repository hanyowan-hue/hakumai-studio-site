2. Device records, voice, and backups

Diary text, photos, comics, characters, and settings are stored in SQLite and local files. Attaching a photo to a diary does not send it to us or AI providers. Copies you export or share are handled at your chosen destination.

On iOS, audio recorded with “Talk” is saved in local files. Transcription uses Apple speech recognition on the device when supported. If the language or device does not support on-device recognition, audio may be sent to Apple’s servers for recognition. Android uses only on-device speech recognition and is unavailable on unsupported devices; the current Android implementation does not save audio files. We do not send audio to our servers or generative AI providers (OpenAI or Anthropic). Text transcribed from speech is sent as described in sections 3 and 4 if you use it for AI generation.

Automatic backups on iOS are saved in your own iCloud Drive, not on our servers. ZIP files are created on your device and passed to your chosen destination when you export or share them. Backups and exported copies are subject to the destination’s policies and your management.

The diary lock using Face ID or your device passcode, reminder notifications, and widgets operate on your device. These features do not send authentication information, notification settings, or displayed diary content to us.

3. Transmission to and storage in Supabase

For comic or character generation, we send the necessary diary text, dialogue, character information, drawing instructions, and reference images to our server (Supabase), with an anonymous user ID. Diary text, drawing instructions, and face and upper-body crops of people’s photos are processed during generation and are not saved in our database or image storage. Photos of children’s drawings are stored as described below.

We store the anonymous user ID, generated comics and character images, extracted features, generation status, errors, timestamps and costs, usage and credit balances, and purchase records (StoreKit transaction and original transaction IDs, product, purchase date, expiry, refund status, and account association). Images are in private storage and accessed through user authentication. These records support result delivery, credits, purchase verification, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting.

For “My child’s drawing,” the photo of the drawing is straightened and perspective-corrected on your device, then stored in private storage on our server as a drawing reference. We reuse it whenever you create comics using that drawing and send it to OpenAI for drawing. The reference stored on our server is also deleted when you delete your account.

4. External AI providers

We send diary text, dialogue, and character information to the Anthropic API to create storyboards. For photo-based characters, we also send a face and upper-body crop to extract features. Generated character images and selected features may also be sent for quality checks.

We send text-derived drawing instructions, dialogue, character features, and reference images, including generated images and children’s drawings, to the OpenAI API for drawing and editing. For characters based on people’s photos, we also send the face and upper-body crop. Before storyboard creation, including the free trial, we send diary text and dialogue to OpenAI’s Moderation API to check for inappropriate content; before “Fix just this with AI,” we send the edit instruction for the same check. These transmissions support requested generation, quality checks, and input safety checks.

Neither provider uses API data for model training by default. OpenAI normally keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions; flagged image inputs may be retained for human review. Anthropic normally deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, with longer retention possible for legal or policy-enforcement reasons. Our not storing face and upper-body crops of people’s photos is separate from provider retention; zero retention is not guaranteed. Official sources: OpenAI, Anthropic (retention), Anthropic (training).

Obtain consent from the people photographed. For children’s photos and children’s drawings, obtain parental or guardian consent and the necessary rights. Face and upper-body crops of people’s photos are processed only to draw characters and are not stored by us. Storage of drawing references is described in section 3 and AI provider retention in section 4.

6. Free trial and DeviceCheck

We use Apple DeviceCheck to check the once-per-device trial, sending a token to Apple to query and update trial status. We store a SHA-256 token hash, trial reservations and usage, and an anonymous user ID; during reservations we temporarily store the encrypted token, never plaintext. Reservations expire after 30 minutes. The encrypted token is cleared on success or when expired reservations are checked. We do not obtain direct identifying details such as the device owner’s name, but use hashes and usage records to prevent trial abuse.
