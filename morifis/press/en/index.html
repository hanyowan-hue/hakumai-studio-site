About the game

Open the forest, build your dungeon, and raise nearly 50 allies to defend it.

Awaken your allies from a forest brimming with life. Enemies march along the paths you open.

Choose where to open, and where to lead the enemy. Shape the terrain however you like, then take on the invaders with your allies. Send every invader back to the forest before the Komorebi Stone is taken, and victory is yours!

Key features

Open the forest and design a dungeon of your own

Work out tactics with a limited number of moves, like solving a puzzle. No two layouts are ever the same.

Collect and raise nearly 50 allies

From spirits to dragons, nearly 50 allies appear as you clear the forest. They change form as they grow, and you fill in the codex one entry at a time.

Raising allies strengthens their special abilities too

When allies level up through training, their special abilities, such as healing and howling, grow stronger along with their HP and attack power.

Conquer five worlds

Travel from sunlit groves to forests of snow and ice. Each world has its own scenery, with new terrain and new enemies waiting.

Several actions to choose from as the battle changes

Opening the forest is only one of the actions players can take. - Open: carve paths through the forest and awaken allies. - Flower: grow a flower that draws enemies in, holding them up while dealing damage. - Root: place a wall of roots that enemies cannot pass, blocking the path. - Tap an ally three times: return extra allies to the Forest Ibuki. Battles play out in real time, and the situation keeps changing. Deciding on the spot which action to use and when is what makes the game fun.

Ally combinations change how you fight

Forest Trial, an endurance mode

The Forest Trial takes place on a large map where two forests sit side by side. Players face an endless stream of invaders and try to see how many Waves they can hold out. Enemies grow stronger as the Waves go on, and every 9 to 12 Waves players move to a new map and open their paths again from scratch. Because players stay on one map for a long time, a forward-looking plan for which allies to raise and when is what decides the outcome. The Wave each player reaches can be compared with the records of players around the world.

Adapting strategy to each enemy's traits

The invading Royal Survey Team is not easy to beat either.

Players watch which enemies are coming and adjust the paths they open and the allies they combine.

Story

Once, a kingdom overflowed with light, until a shadow slowly began to fall over it.

To make the land shine again, the Royal Survey Team is sent to bring back the Komorebi Stone that sleeps deep in the forest.

Footsteps are closing in on the quiet forest.

Together with your allies, can you defend it to the very end?

Developer comment

"I've always loved games where you build and defend your own dungeon, and games where you raise monsters, and I made Morifis because I wanted to take the fun of digging your own paths and defending them with the allies born there, and turn it into something you can play anytime on a smartphone," said the developer of Hakumai Studio. "Depending on how you combine your allies, even the same stage can be fought in a completely different way. I'd be glad if players find a way to win that's all their own. Of everything in the game, my favorite is the Forest Trial, where you see how many Waves you can hold out. It's fairly tough for a mobile game, but that also makes it something you can keep playing for a long time."

About Hakumai Studio

Hakumai Studio is a one-person indie studio based in Japan. Morifis is its first game, with everything from design and development to live operation handled by a single developer.
