Open the forest, build your dungeon, and raise nearly 50 allies to defend it. Every path you open becomes your defense line in this real-time tower defense strategy game.

Awaken your allies from a forest brimming with life. Every path you open becomes a corridor built to meet the invaders.
Choose where to open, and where to lead the enemy. Shape the terrain however you like, and fight alongside the allies you've awakened. Send every invader back to the forest before the Komorebi Stone is taken, and victory is yours!

Key features
- Open the forest and design a dungeon of your own: A puzzle-like strategy simulation where every move counts. No two layouts are ever the same.
- Collect and raise nearly 50 allies: From spirits to dragons. Evolutions that transform them as they grow, and a codex you fill one entry at a time.
- Conquer five worlds: From sunlit groves to forests of snow and ice, each with its own terrain and enemies.
- Read the battle and take control: Step into the real-time battle whenever you like.

About Hakumai Studio
Hakumai Studio is an independent studio run by a single developer in Japan. Morifis is the studio's first game, planned, developed and operated by one person.
